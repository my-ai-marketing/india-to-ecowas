Vegetable processing · India to West Africa

Process near supply.
Export the value.

A 1,000 sqm vegetable processing and dehydration unit can be technically established in India for ECOWAS exports. The investment case, however, depends less on port rent and more on farm-direct procurement, fresh-to-dry yield, energy efficiency and achievable B2B selling prices.

02 / Project framework

One plant model.
Three location strategies.

All locations are compared on the same 5-tonne/day fresh-input model so rent, sourcing and port economics remain comparable.

01

Capacity

5 tonnes/day fresh vegetables, 26 operating days per month, approximately 1,560 tonnes/year at full utilization.

02

Products

Mushroom, green beans, carrots, green onions, corn and green pepper, initially sold as B2B ingredients rather than consumer FMCG.

03

Processing

Receiving → washing → grading → cutting → blanching where needed → dehydration → QA → bulk export packing.

04

Plant area

Approximately 1,000 sqm rented industrial facility with food-grade conversion, cold holding and truck access.

05

Key constraint

Fresh vegetable cost and drying recovery dominate the economics. A small error in yield can outweigh annual rent differences.

06

Go-to-market

Target importers, food manufacturers, seasoning companies, soup producers and B2B repackers across selected ECOWAS markets.

11 / Product economics

Treat six vegetables as six businesses.

The factory should not launch all six SKUs at equal volume. Each requires its own fresh-price, trim loss, dehydration yield, energy curve, quality specification and buyer price.

Pilot first

Carrot

Mechanically straightforward and suitable for early process validation. Tamil Nadu has established carrot production, including Dindigul.

Pilot first

Green beans

Requires quality control for maturity, fibre, diameter, colour and rehydration performance.

Validate premium

Green onion

Very high moisture makes yield critical, but flakes can serve soup and seasoning buyers.

Validate premium

Green pepper

Colour retention and final grade strongly affect selling price.

Compare process

Corn

Commercial study should compare dehydrated sweet corn with IQF/frozen economics.

Specialist

Mushroom

High raw-material value plus high moisture can create expensive dried output. Require buyer-backed pricing before scale.

Simple screening formula

Fresh cost ÷ dry recovery.

A fresh input at /kg with 15% recovery contributes about /kg of dried-product raw material cost. At /kg and 10% recovery, raw material alone becomes /kg before processing.

15 / Implementation roadmap

Spend in stages.
Release capital against evidence.

01

Supplier validation

Map 5–10 suppliers per priority SKU; collect seasonal price/volume and transport quotations.

02

Pilot drying trials

Measure cleaned yield, trim loss, drying hours, energy use, final moisture, water activity, colour and rehydration.

03

Buyer validation

Send samples to ECOWAS importers/processors and obtain written target specifications and price indications.

04

Site & compliance due diligence

Check food use, power, water, drainage, fire, pollution-consent route and transport access before lease.

05

Vendor tender

Obtain at least three comparable processing/dehydration proposals with performance guarantees.

06

Commercial launch

Start with 2–4 validated SKUs and scale only after contribution margin and repeat orders are demonstrated.

16 / Recommendation

Proceed with the study.
Do not yet release full CAPEX.

Current direction

Dindigul / Madurai processing corridor → VOC Port, Tuticorin

For a dehydration-led operation, inland processing deserves first investigation because it can reduce movement of high-moisture fresh produce while retaining access to a competitive export port.

Benchmark alternativeSurat / Sachin → Hazira / west-coast ports
Port-side alternativeTuticorin / Thoothukudi

Full-CAPEX gate2–3 buyer LOIs / trial orders + proven SKU yields
Investment committee conclusion

Commercial feasibility is conditional, not negative.

The plant can be built and operated. The remaining question is whether selected SKUs can consistently realize enough margin after farm-gate cost, drying losses, energy, export logistics and buyer payment terms. Validate that before committing the full line.
